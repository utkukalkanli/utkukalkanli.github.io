About Me

ML / AI Engineer in Munich working across LLM, RAG and agent systems, production MLOps on Azure, and computer vision.

Over three years at Linde GmbH I built the document ingestion and retrieval pipeline of an internal document-processing agent used by ~50,000 employees, maintained and optimized a production Azure ML pipeline that forecasts CPAP therapy adherence for ~1M patients daily (runtime cut from 37 to 8 hours), and delivered an RF-DETR small-object detector for satellite imagery.
